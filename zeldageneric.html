Legend of Zelda NES Dungeon 1 Remake

A remake of the first dungeon from The Legend of Zelda NES with a custom mechanic and level. Our custom mechanic involved a dog companion that can attack enemies, collect items, and trigger pressure plates.

I integrated the health, damage, locked door, and inventory systems, collectibles, various mobs, the bow and boomerang, audio, and interactable environmental objects.

Made using Unity and C#. Please contact me for a copy of a build!
