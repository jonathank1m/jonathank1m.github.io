My Projects

A closer look at the games and projects I've worked on.

LEMON-LIMELIGHT

LEMON·LIMELIGHT is a army commanding puzzle game where you navigate the mind of our protagonist Zestelaine after a break up. Use the limelight to your advantage as you guide your colorful troupe of allies through the fractured-flamed-filed psyche of a troubled soul. Check out the trailer here!

CSE Simulator

Worked on basic navigation, linetrace and affordance system, various interactions (whiteboard, light-switch, trash, exit door, fliers), light flicker, and day-night progression.
Done using blueprint visual scripting in Unreal Engine 5.

A2-Go!

AR location-based mobile app designed for tourists in Ann Arbor. Players explore the city via a map-based interface to discover landmarks, collect resources, and plant virtual trees that contribute to Ann Arbor's goal of becoming the ultimate "Tree City USA." Made using Unity and C#.

Loaded Mice

Rapid prototype of a dice rolling turn-based combat game. Players choose to roll and reroll dice to achieve combos based off of poker hands in order to deal reduce the opposite side's health to zero first.
Programmed event handling, scoring, and interaction system. Made using Unity and C#.

Legend of Zelda NES Dungeon 1 Remake

I worked on the health and damage system, collectibles, the inventory system, half of the mobs, the bow and boomerang, the lock system, audio, and pushable blocks.
Done using Unity and C#.

Other games I've worked on also include Florida Man and Masqcoluerade.
